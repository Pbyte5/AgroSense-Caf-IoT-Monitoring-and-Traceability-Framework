# AgroSense Café

**IoT Monitoring and Traceability Framework for Colombian coffee production.**

AgroSense Café connects farm operations, environmental monitoring, agronomic
insights, and coffee traceability in one platform. It is designed to help
cooperatives and coffee producers make informed decisions while preserving a
reliable record of each coffee lot's journey from farm to export.

## What the platform does

- **Monitors farms and crops:** Farm, plot, and sensor data provide a shared
  view of growing conditions and operations.
- **Turns telemetry into timely insights:** Weather and IoT readings support
  alerts for risks such as coffee rust, water deficit, and extreme rainfall.
- **Tracks coffee origin:** Traceability links export lots to their contributing
  farms, including small contributions.
- **Supports trustworthy data:** Validation and quality controls identify
  incomplete, inconsistent, or anomalous data before it informs decisions.

## How it fits together

AgroSense brings together farm and producer records, field sensor telemetry,
weather and soil information, and post-harvest and export records. Data quality
checks prepare these inputs for operational alerts, analytics, and traceability
reports. This gives agronomists, cooperatives, and buyers a clearer view of
production and coffee origin.
